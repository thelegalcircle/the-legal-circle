Editorial information

How The Legal Circle publishes.

The Legal Circle publishes legal news, analysis, explainers, interviews and practical perspectives for legal professionals.

Editorial responsibility

Articles carrying the institutional byline The Legal Circle are prepared and published by The Legal Circle’s editorial team. The Legal Circle is operated by Adelia Salikhova, who is responsible for the publication’s editorial direction. Magneo designed and built the website but does not operate the publication or review its submissions.

Reporting and analysis

We identify the relevant jurisdiction and event date, prioritize primary sources and link readers to those sources where available. News reports present verified developments. Analysis, opinion and explainers are labelled so readers can distinguish interpretation from straight reporting.

The website provides general information and does not create a lawyer–client relationship or provide legal advice.

Contributors and attribution

Expert contributors, interview participants and reviewers are credited only when they actually participated. Contributor permissions and publication terms are agreed separately; submitting an idea does not transfer ownership or guarantee publication.

Corrections

We correct material factual errors and add a dated correction note when a change affects the substance of an article. Minor typographical or formatting changes may be made without a note.

To question a statement or request a correction, email networking@thelegalcircle.ca and include the article URL and the information you believe should be reviewed.

Publication checklist

  • Explain what happened and why it matters.
  • Confirm the jurisdiction and relevant event date.
  • Check and link primary sources.
  • Separate reporting from analysis, opinion or attributed expert perspective.
  • Confirm author, publication date and any substantive update date.
  • Use a unique headline, SEO title, description and excerpt.
  • Provide a suitable image and descriptive alternative text when available.
  • Select only genuinely relevant related articles.
