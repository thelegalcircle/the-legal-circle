The evening

Different professions. Shared challenges.

Business decisions rarely sit neatly within finance or law. This evening brings both perspectives together in a relaxed setting.

The evening welcomes CFOs, finance directors, finance managers, controllers, lawyers, accountants, finance project managers and other professionals working where finance, law and business meet.

Come with a colleague or come on your own. There’s a place for you at the table.

Food & conversation

What’s on the table?

A generous serving of plov—a rice dish traditionally cooked with meat, vegetables and spices—alongside new introductions and conversations about the issues shaping your work.

Tax. Trade. Business growth. The economy. Leadership.

And the questions you’d like to hear another professional’s perspective on.

The discussion topics are taking shape, and your suggestions are welcome. Further details will follow as the evening comes together.
