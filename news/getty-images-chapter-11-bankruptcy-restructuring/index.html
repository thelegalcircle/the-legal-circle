Getty Images Files Chapter 11 to Implement a Plan to Go Private

Getty Images has moved from announcing a restructuring to beginning the court process needed to implement it.

By The Legal CircleAnalysisOctober 21, 20262 min read
  • #Getty Images bankruptcy
Business & Practice Development

Analysis

Published by The Legal Circle

October 21, 2026

Delaware, United States; Canadian implications

Getty Images has moved from announcing a restructuring to beginning the court process needed to implement it.

The company and certain US subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 petitions in Delaware on October 21. Getty’s securities disclosure says the proceedings are intended to implement a prepackaged reorganization under its previously announced restructuring support agreement. Getty Images: October 21 Form 8-K, as filed.

The proposed transaction would take Getty private, backed by an equity investment of up to US$533 million from Getty-family interests and affiliates of Koch Equity Development. SEC: announced restructuring and equity commitment.

Filing is not confirmation

The filing is a material development beyond the earlier announcement. It is not confirmation of the plan or completion of the ownership change.

Getty says it has filed motions intended to support ordinary operations, including payments to employees, vendors, suppliers and content contributors. Its disclosure states that the plan contemplates leaving general unsecured claims unimpaired. These remain elements of the proposed court-supervised process. Getty Images: October 21 Form 8-K, as filed.

Identify the entity and obligation

For licensing and restructuring counsel, the practical task is to identify which entity is party to each relationship and how the relevant obligation is treated.

A broad announcement about continued operations cannot answer every question about a particular agreement, payment or claim. Customers and contributors need to examine the applicable documents and subsequent orders.

The distinction between creditor treatment and ownership treatment also deserves attention. A restructuring can propose continuity for commercial relationships while changing the capital structure substantially.

Getty’s filing places those issues within a formal process in which approvals, objections and implementation conditions matter.

What affected businesses should check

For Canadian lawyers advising affected businesses, the useful first step is therefore specific: identify the contracting party, the relevant rights and any notice requiring a response. The brand’s global presence does not establish that every affiliate is a debtor.

The next reporting milestones will be court orders and progress toward confirmation, rather than another description of the announced transaction.

The restructuring now has a court file. Its proposed outcome still requires the legal process.
