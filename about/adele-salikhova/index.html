The people behind The Legal Circle

Adele Salikhova

Founder of Magneo and The Legal Circle · Legal marketing and applied AI

Adele Salikhova is a marketing professional with more than 10 years of experience spanning in-house roles and legal technology companies. She is the founder of Magneo ↗, a marketing business serving regulated industries, and The Legal Circle, a media and community platform for legal professionals.

Her work brings together marketing, professional positioning and the practical application of AI. Through Magneo, she helps businesses communicate their expertise, strengthen their online presence and develop their marketing.

Adele has organised a legal event for 5,000 lawyers and is the author of Personal Branding for Legal Professionals ↗, a practical guide to professional visibility. She is also the author of The Legal Circle’s marketing blog and writes a newsletter for legal professionals.

Through The Legal Circle, she brings this experience into conversations with lawyers and other experts, creating opportunities to exchange perspectives and build professional relationships.
