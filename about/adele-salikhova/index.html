Adele Salikhova

Legal marketing professional · Interviewer · Founder of The Legal Circle

Adele Salikhova is a legal marketing professional with more than 10 years of experience, including in-house roles and work within legal technology companies. She has organised a legal event for 5,000 lawyers, written Personal Branding for Legal Professionals, and writes a marketing blog.

She founded The Legal Circle to bring legal professionals together through news, expert interviews and opportunities to connect. As an interviewer and community creator, she explores professional experiences and practical perspectives relevant to the legal community.
